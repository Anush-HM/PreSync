WHAT PRESYNC DOES

Four systems, one room

Each one solves a different problem. Together, they let a team build and present without ever tripping over each other.

Editors

Edit together, live

Add slides, retype content, move things around — with your team, at the same time, with nothing to save or merge.

Presenter

A stable view for the room

Whatever the presenter shows is snapshotted the instant they arrive on it — so background edits never flash on screen mid-talk.

Viewers

Notes that wait for you

Jot a private note on any slide. If the presenter moves on while you're mid-thought, your view waits until you hit Done.

Q&A

Answers grounded in the deck

Questions get a drafted answer pulled only from what's actually on the slides — or an honest "not covered" if it isn't.

HOW IT WORKS

From upload to live in four steps

Real order matters here — each step depends on the one before it.

01

Upload your deck

Drop in a PDF exported from PowerPoint, Slides, or Keynote. PreSync reads the real text and rebuilds it as editable content — not a flattened picture.

EDITOR
02

Edit together

Retype text, add blocks, reorder slides — with as many teammates as you need, all changes visible instantly, no locking.

EDITOR
03

Go live

Start a session and get a room code. From here, your view is what the room sees — editing continues safely in the background.

Join with a code

No account needed. Enter the code, add a name, and follow along — take notes, ask questions, stay in sync.
